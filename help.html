Use CampusFix like an app

CampusFix is a progressive web app. Install it from your browser for a full-screen experience and static asset caching. Live data still requires a network connection.

What works offline?

The app shell and public pages are cached for offline viewing. Complaint data, notices, and messages are never cached and always require a live connection.

Still need a person?

Use Messages to contact available campus staff. If you cannot sign in, contact your campus administrator directly.
